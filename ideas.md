<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Modern Clinical Serenity
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Clarity & Calm**: The medical experience can be stressful; the design should reduce anxiety through clean lines and calming visuals.
  2. **Professional Trust**: Use structured layouts and high-quality imagery to convey medical expertise and reliability.
  3. **Accessibility First**: Ensure all information is easily readable and navigable for patients of all ages.
  4. **Technological Advancement**: Reflect the clinic's investment in digital imaging and PACS through modern UI elements.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Deep Medical Blue (#1E5A8E) - Represents trust, stability, and professional healthcare.
  - **Secondary**: Teal/Cyan (#2C8C99) - Evokes cleanliness, technology, and healing.
  - **Accent**: Warm Orange (#E67E22) - A subtle nod to the original branding, used sparingly for warmth and key actions.
  - **Backgrounds**: Soft whites and very light grays to maintain a sterile but welcoming feel.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Asymmetric Split Screens**: For hero sections and service highlights to balance imagery with information.
  - **Card-Based Grid**: For services and resources, providing clear, digestible chunks of information.
  - **Sticky Side Navigation (Desktop)**: For quick access to key sections without cluttering the header.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Glassmorphism Cards**: Subtle transparency on service cards to layer content over clean backgrounds.
  - **Rounded Geometry**: Soft corners on buttons and images to appear friendly and approachable.
  - **Linear Icons**: Thin, precise medical icons that reflect diagnostic precision.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Hover Lifts**: Cards gently lift and cast deeper shadows on hover to invite interaction.
  - **Smooth Scrolling**: Navigation links glide to sections rather than jumping.
  - **Clear Feedback**: Buttons ripple or change state clearly upon interaction.

  **Animation**:
  - **Fade-in Up**: Content elements slide gently upward and fade in as the user scrolls.
  - **Pulse Effects**: Subtle pulsing on the "Book Appointment" CTA to draw attention without being aggressive.
  - **Image Parallax**: Gentle movement of background images to add depth.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: Montserrat (Bold/Semi-bold) - Modern, geometric, and highly legible.
  - **Body**: Open Sans (Regular) - Friendly, neutral, and excellent for readability.
  - **Hierarchy**: Clear distinction between H1, H2, and body text using size and weight contrast.
</idea>

<idea>
  **Design Movement**: Community Warmth
  **Core Principles**:
  1. **Human Connection**: Focus on the people (staff and patients) rather than just machines.
  2. **Local Roots**: Emphasize the 30+ years in Kamloops.
  3. **Soft & Organic**: Avoid clinical sterility in favor of a welcoming atmosphere.
  4. **Storytelling**: Use narrative structures to explain services.

  **Color Philosophy**:
  - **Primary**: Sage Green (#5D8C7B) - Natural, calming, and grounded.
  - **Secondary**: Warm Beige (#F5F5DC) - Soft background tone.
  - **Accent**: Terracotta (#E07A5F) - Warm, human, and inviting.

  **Layout Paradigm**:
  - **Magazine Style**: Large typography mixed with overlapping images.
  - **Fluid Shapes**: Organic blobs and waves to break up straight lines.

  **Signature Elements**:
  - **Hand-Drawn Illustrations**: Custom icons that look sketched.
  - **Texture**: Subtle paper or grain textures on backgrounds.
  - **Portrait Photography**: Focus on smiling staff and patients.

  **Interaction Philosophy**:
  - **Gentle Fades**: Transitions are slow and soft.
  - **Parallax Scrolling**: Elements move at different speeds for a natural feel.

  **Animation**:
  - **Float**: Elements gently float in place.
  - **Draw-in**: Lines and shapes draw themselves on scroll.

  **Typography System**:
  - **Headings**: Playfair Display (Serif) - Elegant and human.
  - **Body**: Lato (Sans-serif) - Friendly and approachable.
